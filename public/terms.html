RULES & TERMS · 1 NOVEMBER 2026 · EARLY ACCESS PROTOTYPE

KeepMoving is an experimental GPS-based hide-and-seek game. Never put yourself, another player or anyone nearby in danger to win a round. If someone needs to stop, stop.

1. Who can play

Each participant must knowingly join the game and agree to share their device's location for the match. Players under 18 must obtain permission from a parent or guardian and play with suitable adult supervision. Do not track anyone without their knowledge or consent.

2. The game

  • A host creates a room, selects the centre using device GPS, chooses the radius and time, and assigns Hunters and Shadows.
  • Shadows receive the selected head start. Hunters must not pursue until the hunt phase begins.
  • Hunters see brief GPS location hints at configured intervals. The live map does not guarantee that any marker represents an exact current position.
  • The circular zone contracts at configured times. A GPS boundary warning is not an automatic capture.
  • A Hunter can request a tag when the backend considers the players nearby; the Shadow must confirm the capture on their own phone. Do not force physical contact or interfere with another player's device.
  • Hunters win when every Shadow has been captured. Shadows win if one or more survive the game timer. The host can end a game early.

3. Playing safely

All participants must agree on a safe and permitted play area before starting. Do not trespass, enter restricted buildings, cross roads dangerously, use vehicles for a chase, climb structures, push or tackle people, interfere with strangers, enter railway tracks or visit unsafe or isolated places. Stop the game if weather, traffic, darkness, injury or other circumstances make participation unsafe.

The app accepts a configured radius up to 100 kilometres as a software limit, not a recommendation. A walking game should generally use a much smaller, familiar area.

4. Technical limitations

Phone GPS can drift or become stale. Browser location updates often pause when a phone locks, changes apps or loses connectivity. Map data, roads, timers, reveals, distances and zone boundaries are not emergency-navigation tools. Players are responsible for independently judging whether movement is safe.

5. Fair play

Do not spoof GPS coordinates, misuse another player's session credential, join rooms without permission, harass or stalk anyone, submit false capture confirmations, or use the game to collect other people's whereabouts.

6. Availability and project status

KeepMoving is provided as an early experimental prototype. Games may fail, lag, stop unexpectedly or lose connection. Features, limits, security protections and retention practices may change as the project is developed. No prize, reward, uninterrupted operation or precise tracking is guaranteed.

7. Privacy

Read the privacy notice before enabling GPS. Participants can stop participating and revoke browser location permission at any time.

8. Project questions

See the project repository for information. Do not disclose sensitive GPS data or player credentials in public issues.
